Behind the Studio

I create tactile, story-driven artwork across distinct mediums—ranging from expressive impasto canvas paintings to hand-sculpted wet-felted wool and high-fire ceramic sculptures.

Working out of Las Vegas, every piece explores tension, organic textures, and bold color palettes made to energize living and commercial spaces.
